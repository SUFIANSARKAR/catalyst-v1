# Catalyst Catalyst Bridge — Phases G–H

## Phase G — Device Operating Shells
Catalyst now exposes a provider-neutral device protocol for desktop, Android and web clients. Tauri and Kotlin/Compose shells advertise their capabilities and can share the same Catalyst API/session identity. The server remains the authority for identity, policy and approvals; shells are actuators, not independent brains.

## Phase H — Situational Intelligence
The proactive layer maintains signals, suggestions and a small focus store. It observes mission failures, due automations and connected device presence, then creates **reviewable suggestions**. It does not silently execute proactive actions.

## Design rule
Existing Catalyst missions, approvals, memory, computer use, realtime voice, research and improvement systems remain authoritative. G/H adds an operating shell and proactive layer rather than replacing them.
